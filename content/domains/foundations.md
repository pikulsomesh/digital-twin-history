## The short version

Every twin rests on a few foundations: ways to **simulate** physical behavior, to **fuse** live data with a model, to **represent** things in 3D and as data, and to **agree** on vocabulary and interfaces.

## Simulation

- [Monte Carlo methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_method) (1940s): exploring uncertainty by simulation.
- Finite element analysis and [NASTRAN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nastran) (1968): predicting stress, heat and vibration.
- Machine-learned surrogates and hybrid physics–AI models (2020s): making simulation fast enough to run live ([survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19390)).

## Data fusion

- The [Kalman filter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman_filter) (1960): the predict-measure-correct loop at the heart of synchronization.
- Dynamic data-driven application systems (2000s): models that steer their own data collection ([National Academies](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/read/26894/chapter/4)).

## Representation

- [Sketchpad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sketchpad) (1963) and [solid modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_modeling) (1980s): geometry as data.
- [NeRF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.08934) (2020) and [3D Gaussian splatting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04079) (2023): reality captured as photorealistic 3D.

## Interoperability and standards

- [OPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Platform_Communications) (1996), [ISA-95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANSI/ISA-95) (2000), [OPC UA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPC_Unified_Architecture) (2008), [CityGML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CityGML) (2008).
- [Asset Administration Shell](https://industrialdigitaltwin.org/en/) (from 2016).
- [ISO 23247](https://www.iso.org/standard/75066.html) (2021) and [ISO/IEC 30173](https://www.iso.org/standard/81442.html) (2023).

## Trust

Verification, validation and uncertainty quantification (VVUQ), security and governance are now treated as foundations in their own right ([National Academies, 2023](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/read/26894/chapter/4)).
