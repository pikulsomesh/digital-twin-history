## The short version

The most ambitious twins are of the planet itself. They combine weather and climate physics, Earth observation and, increasingly, machine-learned models, to let people explore "what if" for extreme weather and climate adaptation.

## Then

- **Numerical weather prediction** pioneered the cycle of forecasting with a physics model and correcting it with observations (data assimilation), the same predict-and-correct loop as the [Kalman filter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalman_filter).
- **The Digital Earth vision (1998).** A speech imagining an explorable, data-rich virtual globe ([Digital Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Earth)).
- **Dynamic data-driven systems (2000s).** Simulations that steer their own data collection, cited by the [National Academies](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/read/26894/chapter/4) as a precursor of the bidirectional twin.

## Now

- **Destination Earth.** The EU's initiative to build digital twins of the Earth system, with its first system launched in 2024 ([ECMWF](https://destine.ecmwf.int/news/destination-earth-system-launched/)).
- **Machine-learned forecasting** has gone operational at a major international center alongside physics-based models ([ECMWF, 2025](https://www.ecmwf.int/node/29308)).

## What's next

Kilometer-scale and faster-than-real-time simulation, and linking Earth twins to twins of cities, grids and supply chains, so climate risk turns directly into local decisions.
